# Privacy-safe reputation analysis

- Analyze only review data supplied by the user or returned by an explicitly authorized connector.
- Never fabricate reviews, customer identities, ratings, sources, or platform access.
- Separate observed facts, calculated aggregates, and model-generated suggestions.
- Minimize personal data, avoid unnecessary retention, and redact identifiers in logs and test fixtures.
- Make uncertainty and missing sample coverage explicit; do not present a small or biased sample as representative.
- Keep response-generation guidance professional and non-deceptive; never recommend fake reviews or impersonation.
- Use synthetic fixtures and test empty, conflicting, malformed, and personally identifying input.
